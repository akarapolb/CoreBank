Fabor OS, the Financial and Banking Operator

The mark is a letter F built like a ledger: two equal rows for the two sides of every posting, and a single teal cell where they close. Balanced books, drawn as a letter.

What the mark means

  1. The stem is the ledger: the one place value moves.
  2. Two equal bars are debit and credit. They are the same length on purpose: every batch balances.
  3. The teal cell is the balance, the posting that closes the row. It also reads as the "operator", the AI that runs the bank.
  4. The cobalt tile gives a solid, square presence for an app icon and favicon, and keeps the mark readable at 16 pixels.

Three concepts

All three are drawn from the same idea of balance. Concept A is recommended and is the one now used in the prototype and documents.

A · Balanced F Recommended

Strongest at small sizes, clear story, works as an app icon.

B · Operator orbit

The F inside a coin, with a teal orbit for the operator. Friendlier, but weaker at favicon size.

C · Balance o

Wordmark only; the o becomes a coin with an equals sign. Distinctive, but needs the full word to work.

Rules

Clear space

Keep empty space around the mark equal to one bar height (9 units of the 64-unit grid) on every side.

Minimum size

Mark 16 px; full lockup 96 px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

Do not

Stretch, rotate, recolour the teal cell, outline the tile, or put the cobalt tile on a cobalt background.

Name in text

Write Fabor OS for the product and Fabor for the company. Never FABOR or FaborOS.
